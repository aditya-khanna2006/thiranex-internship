# Customer Segmentation Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on segmenting customers based on their purchasing behavior using transaction data from an online retail business. The objective is to identify distinct customer groups and understand their purchasing patterns, value, and engagement.

The analysis uses **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-learn** to clean the data, perform customer-level analysis, apply K-Means clustering, and generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Identify meaningful customer segments
* Apply K-Means clustering for customer segmentation
* Understand spending and purchasing patterns
* Analyze product preferences across customer segments
* Generate business recommendations for targeted customer strategies

## 📊 Customer Segmentation

Customer behavior was analyzed using the following key features:

* **Recency** — Number of days since the customer's last purchase
* **Frequency** — Number of unique orders placed by the customer
* **Monetary** — Total amount spent by the customer
* **Average Order Value** — Average spending per order

K-Means clustering was used to group customers with similar purchasing behavior.

Based on the clustering analysis, two primary customer segments were identified:

* **High-Value Loyal Customers**
* **At-Risk / Low-Value Customers**

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify differences between customer segments in terms of:

* Purchase frequency
* Recency of purchases
* Total spending
* Average order value
* Product preferences
* Revenue contribution

These insights can help businesses develop targeted strategies for customer retention, engagement, and revenue growth.
